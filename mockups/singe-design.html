Design mockups · variant B

1 · The Singe highlight + popover

The selection becomes a bar of Sorph glass: refracting rim, specular edge, three ribbons turning in 3D inside it, ray-marched like the orb. The bar hugs the term; the text is drawn clean on top, with no distortion or outline. In Demo: hold Left ⌃ (≈350 ms) to open, release to close; clicks inside work while holding; clicking Ask a follow-up keeps it open.

3 · Menu bar: history, saved, lists

Highlight. Same physics as the Sorph orb, flattened into a rounded bar: Fresnel rim tinted by the ribbon hues, a refracting bevel that bends whatever sits under the edge, a specular glint, and an opening animation that overshoots slightly like the orb settling. The bar sits tight around the term, and the term itself is drawn clean on top: no refraction, outline or halo on the letters.
Logo. The same glass bar without text: large mark, wordmark lockup, and the 22 pt menu bar icon.
Menu bar. History (grouped by day, with source app), Saved (starred), and Lists you create (e.g. “DORA”). Colour marks work like Finder tags; sort by Recent, A–Z or Most looked up.
